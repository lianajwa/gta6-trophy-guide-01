Before you start

  • One trophy can be missed. Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy reported as possibly missable: its first meditation spot, at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, is said to vanish after that chapter. Do it in Chapter 1 and keep a manual save.
  • There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Free roam continues after the story.
  • Leave cheats off. Using cheats disables trophies and saving.
  • About 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx), one playthrough, no online trophies. The hardest trophy is A Long Road: 100% completion, with lenient thresholds.
