    Platinum roadmap — six stages, in order

    Built from the official trophy descriptions alone: which story chapters and missions come first, which collectibles and counts come next, and what to clean up last. PowerPyx rates the set 4/10 in difficulty and roughly 200 hours to the platinum — the challenge is length, not skill.

    Missables — the one trophy you can lose

    Out of 55 trophies, exactly one is reported missable. Everything else can be earned after the story, in free roam. This page explains the one exception in full, and why the rest are safe.

    Why the other 54 are safe

    • Story trophies (12): every chapter and mission trophy pops when its mission ends. You cannot skip a story mission, so none of them can be missed.
    • Side-character chains (5): PowerPyx warns that some quest givers, Kenton especially, go temporarily unavailable during the story — but they return. Take their missions when they appear and you lose nothing.
    • Stranger strands: early guides note some stranger chains have windows tied to story progress. Start a strand when it appears rather than hoarding all eight for the endgame.
    • Everything else: activities, collectibles, robberies and one-off stunts are all available in free roam after the story. No trophy needs a second playthrough.

    Audit — how this list was verified

    Verification notes

    • Names, descriptions, grades: Exophase’s trophy list and PSNProfiles’ trophy page agree on all 55. Checked 20 Nov 2026.
    • Hidden count (28): 12 story trophies plus 16 non-story hidden trophies (the five side-character finals, Bret’s hunt, and nine one-off trophies). The split is confirmed by Insider Gaming and HowToVideoGame, which both list the same 28 hidden.
    • Missable count (1): PowerPyx and GTABase both flag Positive Mental Attitude as the only possibly missable trophy; CryptoGames and EGamers report the same. The Chapter 1 balcony meditation is the reason.
    • Online count (0): every source agrees all 55 trophies are offline, single-player.
    • Counts: 42 Shooting Range challenges, 250 stunts, 14 drone-race locations, 9 activity types, 8 stranger strands, 18 meditations, 3 card sets, 100 WAiNK locations, 100 vehicles, 52 areas, 9 gangs, 8 garages, 10 Scooter Bros registrations, 50 challenges, 5 bank vaults, 500 choices — the counts PlayStation tracks, as listed by PSNProfiles and the starter data.
    • What is not yet confirmed: the exact triggers for Personal Best, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Challenging Tank were listed as not yet known by PowerPyx at launch. This guide says so in each trophy’s text rather than inventing a step.
